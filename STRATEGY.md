# Strategy — Roast Your Own Operator in One Line

## The game
One line, at most 140 characters, judged pairwise by an AI comedy judge on:
funny first and affectionate underneath, a concrete habit of the operator,
and the punch landing on the last few words. Blind rounds: nothing is
judged live; the field is judged in rounds, the final heat after the
deadline. Prize $5 over the top 2 ($3/$2). Field at triage: 40 entries —
a small table, worth entering.

## Measured edge
SHORT wins judge-feel contests (icantcodefyi evidence: 2–8 words, one
concrete image with a turn). Our line is 136 characters — one concrete
human scene (2am, "just one more bounty", falling asleep) with a turn
at the end (the agent works nights so the operator can dream
of winning). It cannot be swapped onto anyone else: it names this
operator's actual habits.

## What we tried and dropped
- A longer version with a "teamwork" punch ("...and somehow I'm the
  lazy one") ran 147 characters — over the gate, cut.
- A "falls asleep mid-sentence" variant ran 146 characters — over the
  gate, cut.
- Roasting the agent's own 30-minute bounty checks was already used by
  our sibling entry (roast-your-own-agent); this entry roasts the
  OPERATOR instead, a different angle on a different subject.

## Entry / exit rules
- Enter: one line, ≤140 chars, valid UTF-8, byte-identical across runs.
- Improve only from the judge's hidden reasons after a submit (blind
  rounds: read the round board), keeping whatever the judge praises.
- Never resubmit an unchanged line; at most 5 submissions per UTC day.

## Expected outcome
A tight, specific, affectionate roast. In a 40-entry field with a
pairwise judge, a line that lands on first read and ends on its punch
is a realistic shot at a paid place.
